# Pie Charts

Pie charts are rendered as inline SVG. Define slices as `Label = value` pairs.

## Syntax

```
::: pie
title = Chart Title
Slice A = 40
Slice B = 30
Slice C = 20
Slice D = 10
:::
```

## Example: Resource Distribution

::: pie
title = Resource Distribution
Coal = 35
Iron = 25
Copper = 20
Stone = 15
Sand = 5
:::

## Example: Energy Sources

::: pie
title = Energy Mix
Coal power = 45
Solar = 20
Wind = 15
Hydro = 12
Nuclear = 8
:::

# Research Bench

::: infobox
title = Research Bench
image = static/icons/w98_tree.png
caption = Generates research points
Type = Machine
Category = Research
Input = Items + fuel
Output = Research points
:::

{{stub}}

The **research bench** converts items into research points, unlocking new machines and recipes.

## How it works

Route output items to the bench. It burns coal as fuel and produces research points over time.
